# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

This policy covers the Element Screenshotter browser extension.

## What the extension handles

The extension handles one kind of user data: the content of the web page you are screenshotting. It reads that content only after you start a screenshot on a tab, by clicking the toolbar button, pressing the keyboard shortcut, or choosing **Take Screenshot** from the page's context menu. It uses the content in these ways:

- It captures images of the page and combines them into the screenshot you selected.
- It reads the page's layout to highlight elements under your pointer.
- It uses the page's title in the screenshot's file name.

All of this happens on your device. The screenshot goes only where you send it: to your clipboard when you choose Copy, or to your downloads folder when you choose Download.

The extension also stores its settings, whether you last chose Copy or Download, and where you put the Save buttons. These stay in your browser's extension storage on your device. They contain no page content.

## What the extension doesn't do

- It doesn't send any data over the network. It has no servers, analytics, or advertising.
- It doesn't share or sell data, and doesn't transfer it to anyone.
- It doesn't run code from outside the extension package.
- It doesn't read pages you haven't started a screenshot on.

## Removing data

Screenshots you copied or downloaded are under your control, like any other file. Removing the extension deletes its stored settings.
